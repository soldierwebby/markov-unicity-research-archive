# Markov Number Unicity — Preprints and Research Archive

**Elleard Felix Webster Heffern**

Manuscripts, figures, software, computational verification data, and deposit history from research on Markov-number unicity, also known as the Markov uniqueness or Frobenius conjecture.

## One-trillion-value verification preprint

### Verification of the Markov Uniqueness Conjecture for the First One Trillion Markov Values: Meridians on the Markov Surface and Farey Addresses

**Research preprint — not peer reviewed.**

This preprint reports a computational verification of the Markov uniqueness conjecture for the first **one trillion Markov values in numerical order**, alongside the development of Meridian families on the Markov surface and their connections with Farey addresses.

The trillionth value has **1,021,609 decimal digits**. The general uniqueness conjecture remains open.

- [Read on Hexagon — hexagon:2611.00410](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00410)
- [Read the corrected v22 manuscript on Zenodo — DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24167894](https://zenodo.org/records/24167894)
